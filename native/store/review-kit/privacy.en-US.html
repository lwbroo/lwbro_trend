Privacy policy draft

Unpublished draft. Publisher/copyright owner name Kurt Chiang, public brand, and support email are confirmed; Apple seller verification, policy effective date, and public URL remain pending.

Scope

This draft describes the current approved offline iPhone release of Ziwei Bazi Journal (1.0, build 1). The legal publisher and copyright owner name are confirmed as Kurt Chiang. Apple seller account verification, the effective date, and public URL remain pending. This page is unpublished. Any future AI or sync feature requires an updated policy matching its data flows.

Information entered on your device

An optional name and your entered birth date, time, and gender are used for traditional charts. Dates, mood, energy, tags, notes, and update times are used for journaling and statistics. They are stored in the app on your device; charts and statistics are computed locally.

The offline first release does not send birth information or journal entries to the developer. It has no AI service, app cloud sync, advertising tracking, or automatic diagnostic reporting. Core calendar and chart code is bundled.

Export and sharing you choose

JSON backups contain birth information, journal records, and export information. CSV files contain journal and derived calendar fields. Review content before sharing. Exports are written to a dedicated temporary directory and removed after completion/cancellation; interrupted temporary exports are cleaned at the next startup.

The iOS share sheet delivers files to the destination you choose. A selected app or cloud service may retain, upload, or use the file under its own policies. Clearing app data does not delete saved or shared copies.

Import and restoration

Import reads a selected file locally and does not modify the source file. UTF-8 .json backups up to 5 MB are supported. The whole backup is validated before records are merged by date; a newer update time takes priority, and an existing local birth profile is retained. Cancellation or invalid input does not change existing records.

Retention and deletion

Local birth information and records remain until you edit, delete an entry, or use Settings → Clear All Data. That action removes the birth profile and records in the app. Delete backups separately in Files or the chosen destination. Uninstalling the app or clearing its storage may lose data that was not backed up.

System/device backups may handle app storage according to your settings. No app sync feature is enabled; this does not guarantee that every system backup stays only on the phone.

External links and voluntary contact

A website you choose to open may handle connection information under its own policies. If you voluntarily contact support or send attachments, the recipient receives what you provide. Avoid sending birth details, private notes, or unredacted JSON backups.

The retention period and deletion procedure for support messages require confirmation. No fixed retention period or automatic deletion is promised. The effective date remains unset; this draft is not an effective public policy.

Interpretation and contact

Astrological content is cultural interpretation. Journal charts and historical-average previews describe associations, have no scientifically validated predictive accuracy, and do not provide medical, mental-health, or financial advice. Birthplace time-zone conversion, DST gap/overlap handling and true-solar-time correction are not implemented. International birth data must not be described as producing a corrected chart.
