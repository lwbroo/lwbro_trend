Getting started

1. In Settings, enter a Gregorian birth date, time, and gender; the name is optional. Save to generate charts. 2. Open Birth chart. 3. Select a date in Daily and save mood, energy, tags, and notes. 4. With at least two entries, view Trends. 5. Export a JSON backup before changing devices or removing the app. Use the Language control to choose English or Traditional Chinese. Traditional chart names remain in Chinese.

Why is AI or sync unavailable?

The offline first release has no AI service, app cloud sync, account, or login. Release navigation has no AI, sync or login entry points. This page makes no commitment about a future service or launch date.

Backup and restoration

Export JSON from Entries and choose local Files or a destination you select in the iOS share sheet. Import accepts UTF-8 .json up to 5 MB. CSV is for external viewing and cannot be imported into the app.

Import merges by date, preferring a newer update time and retaining an existing local birth profile. It does not replace the whole database. Keep a backup before intentional data changes.

Why are charts or previews empty?

Trend charts need at least two records. The historical-average day preview needs at least 10 records with the selected metric. Date filters, missing mood/energy values, and small groups affect results. Values are statistical references from your own journal.

Birth times and chart differences

The app uses the date and time as entered, without birthplace time-zone conversion, DST gap/overlap handling or true-solar-time correction. Global availability does not establish validated worldwide calculations. Daily calculations refer to noon on the selected date. Solar-term transition times and different chart rules or traditions can produce different results. Interpretations are cultural content.

Deletion and import problems

Settings → Clear All Data removes the birth profile and journal records in the app. Delete saved backups separately in Files or the destination. If import fails, retain current data and backups, cancel the picker, and check the format and size. Do not uninstall as a restoration test.
