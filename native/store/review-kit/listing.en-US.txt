description
Core screens support English and Traditional Chinese. Detailed traditional chart terms, including star, palace and stem/branch names, remain in Chinese. CSV column headings also remain Chinese.

Ziwei Bazi Journal brings traditional Chinese charts and your personal daily notes together for reflection.

• View Ziwei Doushu and Bazi natal charts, plus calendar-based information for a selected date.
• Record mood and energy on a 1–5 scale, with tags and notes. Edit or delete entries as needed.
• With at least two entries, view mood and energy history and grouped historical averages.
• With at least 10 entries containing the selected metric, a day preview compares historical averages from similar calendar groups.
• Keep data on your device. Export JSON or CSV with the iOS share sheet, and import JSON backups to merge records.

Core features work offline. This version has no AI service, cloud sync, account login, or in-app purchases. A destination you select for sharing may process the file under its own policies.

Birth date and time are used as entered. Birthplace time-zone conversion, DST gap/overlap handling and true-solar-time correction are not implemented. International birth data should not be treated as a corrected chart. Daily calculations use noon on the selected date.

Astrological interpretations are cultural content. Charts and historical-average previews describe associations in your journal; they have no scientifically validated predictive accuracy and do not provide medical, mental-health, or financial advice.
