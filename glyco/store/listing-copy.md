## Full description (both stores — Play allows more length, this fits both)

```
Same meal. Smarter order. Steadier glucose.

Research shows that *the order* you eat your food in — not just what you eat — affects
how much your blood glucose spikes after a meal. Fiber and vegetables first, protein
and fat next, starches and sugars last: this sequence alone can measurably blunt the
post-meal spike, no diet change required.

GlycoOrder turns that research into something you can actually use at every meal.

HOW IT WORKS
1. Snap a photo of your meal (or pick one from your library)
2. GlycoOrder identifies each food and estimates portions
3. Get a step-by-step eating order, with the reason for each step
4. See each food's glycemic index (GI), portion, and carbs

BUILT-IN GI DATABASE
~190 common foods (Western & Asian) with GI values referenced from the University of
Sydney GI Database and nutrition literature. Foods outside the database fall back to
an AI estimate, clearly marked so you always know which is which.

BUILD YOUR OWN EVIDENCE
Log meals, then note whether you followed the suggested order and how you felt an
hour or two later — optionally including your glucose reading if you track it. Over
time, the Trends tab turns your own log into personal evidence: does following the
order actually help you? See your average feeling and glucose broken down by whether
you followed the order, plus both metrics' trend over time.

ALSO INCLUDED
• Today strip — today's meals, carbs, and glycemic load at a glance, with a logging
  streak to keep you consistent
• Burn it off — when today's glycemic load runs high, concrete exercise suggestions
  sized to the excess
• Full data export (JSON) and delete-everything, anytime
• English and Traditional Chinese (繁體中文)
• Light, dark, and auto theme

PRIVACY
No account or sign-in required. Your meal log stays on your device — it's never
uploaded anywhere. Meal photos are sent to our server only to identify the foods in
them, and are not stored. Full details: [privacy policy URL]

NOT MEDICAL ADVICE
GlycoOrder provides general dietary-order suggestions only. It is not a substitute
for guidance from your doctor or dietitian, particularly if you have diabetes or
another condition affecting blood glucose.

FREE TO START
5 meal analyses per week, free, forever — including the Trends dashboard, which stays
free for everyone. Upgrade for unlimited analyses.
```
