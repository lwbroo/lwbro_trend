Glyco Navigator — Privacy Policy

Last updated: July 13, 2026

Glyco Navigator ("the app") helps you decide the order to eat a meal in based on a photo. This page explains what data the app handles and why. It is written to be read in full — there is no separate summary that says something different.

No account, no sign-in

Glyco Navigator does not ask for your name, email address, or any account. On first launch the app generates a random, anonymous device identifier (a UUID) and stores it on your device. That identifier — not you — is what the backend and the subscription system recognize.

What a meal photo goes through

When you analyze a meal, the photo is sent to Glyco Navigator's backend server, which forwards it to Anthropic's Claude API for one purpose only: identifying the foods in the photo and estimating portions. The photo is not stored on the server — it is processed for that single request and discarded. Anthropic processes the image per its own API terms but the request carries no name, email, or account identity, only the anonymous device ID.

Everything else stays on your device. Your meal log — photos, foods, the order you were given, and any follow-up notes you add (how you felt, glucose reading, whether you followed the order) — is stored locally in the app's own storage. It is never uploaded anywhere. You can export it as a JSON file or delete it at any time from Settings.

Weekly usage quota

The free tier allows 5 photo analyses per week. The backend counts how many you've used this week, keyed by your anonymous device ID, so it can enforce that limit and tell you when it resets. This counter self-expires roughly 12 days after it's created — there's no long-term history kept.

Subscriptions

If you subscribe to remove the weekly limit, the purchase is handled entirely by Apple's App Store or Google Play — Glyco Navigator never sees your payment details. RevenueCat, a subscription-management service, links your anonymous device ID to your purchase so the app can check whether you're subscribed. RevenueCat's own privacy practices are described at revenuecat.com/privacy.

What we don't do

  • No advertising, no ad networks, no ad identifiers
  • No analytics or tracking SDKs of any kind
  • No selling or sharing of data with third parties beyond what's described above (the AI recognition call and the subscription platforms)
  • No cross-app or cross-site tracking

Medical disclaimer

Glyco Navigator provides general dietary-order suggestions only and is not medical advice. It is not a substitute for guidance from your doctor or dietitian, particularly if you have diabetes or another condition affecting blood glucose.

Children

Glyco Navigator is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect data from them.

Data retention & deletion

Your local meal log persists until you clear it in Settings or uninstall the app. Server-side, the weekly quota counter and subscription-status cache are anonymous, short-lived, and auto-expiring as described above — deleting the app does not require any separate request to us, but if you'd like your anonymous device ID's server-side counters removed sooner, contact us at the address below.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the "Last updated" date above will change and, for any material change, we'll note it in the app's release notes.
